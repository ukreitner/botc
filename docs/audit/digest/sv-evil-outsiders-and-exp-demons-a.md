## Group notes

1. **`DemonKillPanel` is this group's dominant P0.** 4 of 5 Demons (Al-Hadikhia, Kazali on night 1, Legion, Leviathan, Lil' Monsta) are offered a working one-target Demon kill they must not have — the identical root cause as the user's Pukka complaint (`NightScreen.kt:518-523` → `:534-638`). The fix is the declared per-character `NightAction` + a `demonHasFirstNightKill(id)` / `demonKills(id)` gate, not more `when` branches.
2. **Two Demons break the "one seat, one holder" assumption.** Lil' Monsta is *seatless* (NightPlan must emit a group step with `holderId = null` because the GAME has the character, not a seat — today `NightOrder.kt:143-145` drops it); Legion is *multi-holder* (the actor predicate must be "any Legion alive", not `playerIds.first()`). Both also need `BagShape` overrides that `TEAM_WARPING_IDS` cannot express.
3. **Registration must become a set, and it is the second-biggest cross-cutting gap.** Legion registers as {DEMON, MINION}; the Lil' Monsta babysitter registers as DEMON *on top of* its own character. `Identity.registersAs(p) = p.characterId` (setup-and-identity §B.2) returns one id and `EffectKind.REGISTERS_AS` (status-model) is a per-effect flag — neither expresses this. **Decision needed:** adopt `Registration.registersAs(state, lookup, p): Set<Team>` consumed by InfoCalc (Investigator, Clockmaker, Town Crier, Fortune Teller, Sage, Knight, Flowergirl) and WinCheck.
4. **`ExecutionRecord` is defined twice** — day-engine (`ExecutionOutcome`/`ExecutionVia`/`preventedBy`) vs status-model (`ExecutionKind`/`diedEventId`). These cards use **day-engine's**, because Leviathan, Mutant, Cerenovus and Klutz all need "executed but did not die" plus ST-initiated executions outside nominations. The lead must collapse the two.
5. **Day ledger:** `DayEntry` (day-engine) vs `LedgerEntry` (records-and-memory). Per the brief these cards use `LedgerEntry`; `DayEntryKind.{KLUTZ, MADNESS, GOSSIP…}` map onto `(LedgerKind, sourceId)` pairs. Klutz/Mutant/Cerenovus depend on it existing at all.
6. **One character-change engine serves Barber, Pit-Hag and Kazali.** `changeCharacter(..., reason, newEvil = null, shownCharacterId, suppressReveal)` (setup-and-identity §C.1) is the implementation; `NightEffect.BecomeCharacter` is its declarative face. Non-negotiable: alignment persists (pithag P0 #1 — today a good player turned Evil Twin becomes evil to the whole engine), the outgoing character's tokens die grimoire-wide, once-per-game resets, and a first-night re-run is queued.
7. **Token-source discrimination.** `Mad` (cerenovus / harpy / generic) and `Cursed` (witch / generic) are matched on the **label alone** today, so generic tokens produce false Cerenovus/Witch warnings. Every consumer must match `(sourceId, label)`, and `TokenRule.impairs` must replace the "contains poison/drunk" substring test.
8. **Two missing token primitives.** (a) *Exclusive group*: Leviathan `Day 1…Day 5` (at most one member anywhere) — `placeExclusiveReminder` only clears the identical label, so all five pile up. (b) *Permanent multi-copy*: `sweetheart:Drunk`, `leviathan:Good Player Executed` ×2, `alhadikhia:1|2|3` must never be exclusive-by-accident.
9. **`Ref` cannot address an individual pick.** `NightEffect.SwapCharacters(a: Ref, b: Ref)` (Barber) and Al-Hadikhia's three ordered per-target stages both need `Ref.TARGET_N(i)` or index-addressed effects. Add it before any of these are coded.
10. **Day-triggered characters have no home.** Klutz and Mutant have no night row, and `night_guide.json` is keyed `first`/`other` only, so their entire run-book is unrepresentable. They need `NightGuideEntry.day` (or `day_guide.json`, which travellers-B also asks for) plus `Prompt(at = DAY_START)`; until then they are structurally invisible no matter how good the data is.
