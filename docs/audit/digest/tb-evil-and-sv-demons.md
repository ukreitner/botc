Sources: `docs/audit/characters/{imp,baron,poisoner,scarletwoman,spy,butler,drunk,recluse,saint,fanggu,nodashii,vigormortis,vortox}.md`.
Vocabulary: `mechanics/{night-engine,status-model,day-engine,records-and-memory}.md` + `ux/setup-and-home.md` (`SetupTask`, `bluffSets`).
Totals: **P0 = 22, P1 = 57.**

## Group notes

1. **One root cause, four P0s: `step.playerIds.firstOrNull()`.** `NightScreen.kt:467` (holder) and `:837` (InfoCalc holder) pick the *first seat by index*, so after an Imp star pass / Fang Gu jump / Scarlet Woman promotion the **dead** ex-Demon can own the step and the kill panel vanishes (imp 3, fanggu 1, scarletwoman 3), and a Drunk sharing a row with the real character silently loses its info panel (drunk 1). night-engine §3's `canAct` holder rule **plus** per-holder panels (one `NightAction` + one `ShowInfo` per `playerIds` entry) closes all four. Per-holder rendering is not in night-engine's spec — it only fixes holder *selection*; the plan must add it.
2. **Second root cause, six P0s: effects placed by a malfunctioning source stay live.** poisoner 1 (poisoned Monk still "protects"), drunk 2 (Drunk Monk's `Safe`), nodashii 1 (impaired No Dashii still poisons), scarletwoman 4 (impaired SW still promoted), saint/imp knock-ons. `poisoner.md` and `drunk.md` both propose `PlacedReminder.inert: Boolean` — **reject it** and use status-model §2 `Effect.endsWithSource` + the `abilityWorks(pid, cap)` recursion, which is strictly more correct (it re-evaluates when the source is later cured). *conflict: `inert` snapshots at placement time; `endsWithSource` re-derives — the Poisoner audit's "evaluate impairment at the time the token was placed" intent is lost, and that is the right thing to lose.*
3. **`derivedPoison` must become a `StandingRule` (status-model §1) — three characters depend on it.** `nodashii` (2 nearest-Townsfolk neighbours), `vigormortis` (1 Townsfolk neighbour per `HAS_ABILITY` Minion, ST picks the direction), and the same scan is what `nodashii`'s Spy-override needs. It must **not** recurse into `isImpaired` (nodashii test 4); status-model's `cap` mechanism already guarantees termination.
4. **`deathNotes` is cause-blind and only rendered on 1 of 4 kill paths.** vigormortis 1 (fires on *any* Minion death, incl. execution), saint 1 (never fires), scarletwoman 2 (Day-tab Execute shows nothing), fanggu 8 (fires the *wrong* advice on a jump death). Fixed structurally by `killOutcome(state, lookup, targetId, KillCause)` + the single `GameActions.execute` funnel + the status-model §5 `DeathTrigger` table. `deathNotes` survives only as `killOutcome(...).announce`-style shims.
5. **Misregistration must become a recorded ruling with computed alternatives — no mechanics spec covers this.** spy 4/5, recluse 1–6, nodashii 4, fanggu (Spy-as-Outsider jump), vortox (Recluse-as-Demon makes a "false" answer arguably true). `spy.md` and `recluse.md` both propose `GameState.misregistrations: List<Misregistration>` → translate to `LedgerEntry(kind = RULING, sourceId = "misregister", actorId = spySeat, characterIds = [...])` + `Memory.ruling(state, playerId, askedBy)` (records-and-memory §B/§D5, which already names this exact case). The **new** surface is `InfoResult.alternatives: List<Alternative>` on `InfoCalc` — required by 3 separate needs (below).
6. **`InfoResult.alternatives` has three consumers with different semantics; build one API, three modes.** (a) *impaired* (poisoner 8, drunk 4) — plausible lies, true answer still legal; (b) *misregistration* (recluse 1–4, spy 5) — answers that are true under a different registration, and tapping one **writes the ruling**; (c) *Vortox* (vortox 3) — the true answer must be **un-showable** and every alternative must be provably ≠ true. `NightScreen.kt:904-906` currently gates the false-info chips on a poison/drunk/Vortox substring match, so a misregistration caveat alone produces **no** help at all.
7. **Grimoire-centre / game-global tokens.** `fanggu:"Once"` is officially a centre token; `NightScreen.kt:496` puts it on a seat via `placeExclusiveReminder`, so it *migrates* on a second jump. `fanggu.md` proposes `GameState.fangGuJumped: Boolean` → translate to records-and-memory §C `GameState.storytellerReminders: List<PlacedReminder>` (the general mechanism), with `Memory.isSpent`/a `SPENT` LedgerEntry as the query.
8. **Multi-copy + linked tokens.** `vigormortis` needs 3× `Has ability` and 3× `Poisoned` (night-engine §4 already lists this data fix) **and** a link from each `Poisoned` back to its source Minion for per-Minion teardown → records-and-memory's `PlacedReminder.targetPlayerId`. `vigormortis.md` proposes `linkedPlayerId` — same field, different name.
9. **Two type conflicts the ARCHITECTURE pass must settle, both load-bearing here.** (a) `ExecutionRecord`: status-model's (`kind`, `diedEventId`, `preventedBy`) vs day-engine's (`outcome`, `via`, `preventedBy`, `diedInsteadId`, `tally`). **Adopt day-engine's** — vortox 2 and saint 6 both hinge on `ExecutionOutcome.SURVIVED` being a first-class recorded row, which day-engine's `execute(outcome = SURVIVED)` gives and status-model's null-`diedEventId` expresses less clearly. (b) `DayEntry` (day-engine) vs `LedgerEntry` (records-and-memory) — **adopt `LedgerEntry`**; every day-side need in this group (`GOBLIN_CLAIM`-style claims, misregistration rulings, Gossip-shaped statements) maps to `LedgerKind` + `sourceId`.
10. **What the plan must decide for this group:** whether `NightAction` resolution is per-step or per-holder (see 1); whether the Vortox's false-info constraint is *enforced* (hide the true answer) or *advised*; and whether an impaired Vortox/No Dashii loses its standing clause (both character files say yes, both disagree with shipped `night_guide.json` prose).
